    <p>I am a <strong>Ph.D. candidate in Economics</strong> at Peking University's Guanghua School of Management. My research sits at the intersection of macroeconomics, monetary economics, and finance. I study how policy communication and economic expectations shape financial markets and economic activity.</p>
    <p>My job market paper develops a measure of China's fiscal stance from official communications and studies its effects on asset prices and the macroeconomy. In related work, I use large language models to study economic disagreement and equity return forecasting.</p>
